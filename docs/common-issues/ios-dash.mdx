---
title: "iOS Dashes"
description: "Why --flags still work on iPhone."
icon: apple
---

iPhones and iPads automatically turn two dashes (`--`) into a long dash (`—`) because of
**Smart Punctuation**. Most bots then ignore your flags.

Blep accepts `—`, `–` and `--` for every flag, so `,antinuke ban on —do ban` works exactly like
`,antinuke ban on --do ban`. You don't need to change any settings.

If you want to turn it off anyway: **Settings → General → Keyboard → Smart Punctuation**.
