## Role hierarchy

Discord only lets Blep act on members and roles **below its own top role**. If Blep says a
member is above it, open **Server Settings → Roles** and drag Blep's role higher.

You also can't moderate someone whose top role is equal to or above yours (unless you own
the server).

## Mutes and locks that don't work

If members can still talk after being muted or after `,lock`, the channel or server is giving
them **Send Messages** directly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the channel">
    In the channel's **Permissions** tab, set **Send Messages** for `@everyone` (or your member
    role) to neutral (`/`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check role overrides">
    On Discord, a role **allow** on a channel beats another role's **deny**. If `@Member` has
    Send Messages allowed on the channel, the `Muted` role's deny can't win. Set it to neutral.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Re-run setupmute">
    Channels created after `,setupmute` don't have the mute overrides yet. Running
    `,setupmute` again re-applies them everywhere.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Lockdown with a custom member role

If your members get a role (e.g. `@Member`) instead of relying on `@everyone`, tell Blep to lock
that role:

```javascript Syntax
,lockdown role (role)
```
